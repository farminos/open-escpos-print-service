This is an Android app that provides a PrintService for label and receipt printers.

It supports ESC/POS printers like the Netum G5 or MTP-II. It also supports Citizen printers using the CPCL protocol like the CMP-30.

You can connect printers through Bluetooth, USB or a TCP socket.
